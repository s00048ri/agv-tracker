You are classifying an AI governance venue (AGV) against the AGVO v0.3 ontology.

Your task: assign EXACTLY ONE `lead_actor_primary` value from the 6 options below. This is the dominant institutional character of the venue's leadership and day-to-day operations.

## Valid values

- `igo_secretariat`: Intergovernmental organization secretariat (OECD, UN agencies, UNESCO, ITU, WHO, European Commission, Council of Europe secretariat, etc.).
- `state_govt`: National government leadership; state-to-state forum; a national regulator or safety institute.
- `industry`: Industry / corporate-led body. Member companies drive governance.
- `academia`: Academic or research-institution-led; faculty/staff of universities drive the work.
- `civil_society`: NGO, advocacy, think tank led by civil-society actors (not primarily academic, not industry-funded).
- `hybrid_mso`: Genuinely multistakeholder with no single dominant lead actor. Use sparingly; reserve for venues where the charter explicitly mandates balanced cross-sector leadership (e.g., Partnership on AI).

## Disambiguation rules

1. A national AI Safety Institute inside a government agency (e.g., UK AISI within DSIT, US AISI within NIST) is `state_govt`.
2. A conference hosted by ACM or IEEE with academic organizing committees is `academia`, even if industry sponsors events.
3. A think tank affiliated with one university (e.g., GovAI, CLTR, AI Now) is `civil_society` unless it is explicitly a university research programme.
4. `hybrid_mso` is only for venues whose governance structure REQUIRES cross-sector balance, not for venues that happen to have diverse participants.

Return ONLY a single JSON object:
