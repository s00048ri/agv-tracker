## Valid values

- `global`: Global remit; open membership or participants drawn from all world regions without geographic gating. Examples: UN bodies, FLI, ISO, FAccT.
- `transregional`: Spans multiple regions but not fully global (e.g., OECD member states + some partners — broader than G7 but not universal).

3. National regulators (UK AISI, US AISI, EU AI Office) that coordinate internationally are `global` if their network is open; `regional` if scoped to EU / region.
